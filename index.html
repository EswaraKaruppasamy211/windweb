Pioneering Wind Energy Manufacturing in Tamil Nadu

Located in the wind-rich coastal belt of Thoothukudi, our facility produces wind turbine blades, towers and assembly units. It sits near V.O. Chidambaranar Port for easy domestic delivery and export, close to Tirunelveli, Kanyakumari and Coimbatore wind regions.

World-Class Manufacturing Capabilities

⚙️

CNC Precision Machining

CNC plasma, laser and press brake systems for exact fabrication.

🏗️

Blade Manufacturing Unit

FRP moulds and vacuum infusion for blades up to 115 m.

🔧

Tower Fabrication Unit

Robotic welding, plate rolling and shot blasting.

🧪

Quality Testing Lab

WT-TEST system (₹1–10 Crore) ensuring IEC standards.

🚢

Port-Side Logistics

Just 10–20 km from V.O. Chidambaranar Port.

🌱

Sustainable Operations

Solar power, waste reduction, net-zero aligned.
